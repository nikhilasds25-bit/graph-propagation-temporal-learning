The saved max_earliest_arrival_witness_hops counts hops in the actual foremost-path witnesses retained by earliest-arrival relaxation. It is not the maximum possible temporal walk and is not guaranteed to be the globally fewest-hop foremost path: an earlier intermediate arrival can dominate a later shorter path. Reachability and arrival times are exact under the specified non-decreasing timestamp definition; witness depth is descriptive only. This clarification supersedes the phrase shortest witness in the initial temporal report.
