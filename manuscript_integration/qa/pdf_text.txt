Abstract
We examine directed interaction structure, hypothetical propagation, and weekly orientation
forecastinginanIndianpoliticalTwitterdatasetcontaining85,495tweetsand109,443suppliednet-
workrows. Literalmatchingyieldsalargestweaklyconnectedcomponentof17,598usersand29,715
directed edges, but its largest strongly connected component contains only 17 users. Text-visible
references reconstruct 129,604 timestamped events across 109,219 unique directed pairs. Temporal
ordering further constrains reach: among 500 high-out-strength seeds, mean temporal/static reach
is 74.87% in recorded interaction direction and 52.38% under a layer-aware directional sensitivity.
These ratios vary by seed stratum. In a balanced 25-step simulation with 25 initial seeds per class,
the full recorded-direction model produces only 0.2695 secondary adoptions per run, or 0.00539
per initial seed. Only 0.05896% of non-seed nodes encounter competing exposure opportunities,
with 0.0755 successful competition resolutions per run. Across 15 forecasting test weeks, graph-
only balanced accuracy remains near 0.50; Persistence reaches 0.5403 and history-based models
approximately 0.5535. Reversed graph attention does not improve over a matched history-plus-
structuralMLP.Theevidencesupportsrestricteddirectedrecurrence, chronology-dependentreach,
seed-dominated simulated spread, and modest predictive value from behavioral history; it does not
establish causal contagion or broad graph-model superiority.
1 Introduction
Political discussion on social media involves recorded interactions, repeated user behavior, and po-
tential exposure to information. These are related but distinct observations. A directed path can
describe a possible structural connection without verifying transmission, and a user’s future dataset
label may be predictable from prior behavior without revealing contagion. This study evaluates these
distinctions in a directed Indian political Twitter network.
The empirical graph is weakly connected but with only a very small strongly connected core (17
users). Its binary tweet label is operational: throughout this paper, class 0 denotes Target 0 and
class 1 denotes Target 1. Neither class is treated as verified political stance, misinformation, harmful
content, or truth. The supplied edge table has no timestamps, so temporal analysis uses interactions
reconstructed from visible text references. The recorded direction is tweet author → text-referenced
account, whose interpretation as information flow requires caution.
The study addresses three questions:
RQ1: How do directed topology and interaction chronology restrict potential reach?
RQ2: How much secondary adoption and direct competition occur under specified transmission,
direction, and seed-allocation assumptions?
1
RQ3: Does graph aggregation improve weekly forecasting beyond structural features, Persistence,
and historical orientation?
We combine population audits, typed interaction reconstruction, time-respecting reachability, con-
trolled stochastic simulations, and rolling-origin forecasting. The contribution is an empirical assess-
ment of information boundaries and conditional model behavior. Forecasting and simulated adoption
are evaluated separately; prediction performance is not evidence of observed propagation.

3.3 Reconstructing temporal interactions
Interactions are extracted from visible text patterns: “RT @user” indicates a text-supported retweet,
a leading handle indicates an inferred reply, and remaining handles indicate mentions. A target is
counted once within a tweet, using retweet > inferred reply > mention priority. Case-insensitive
handles are mapped to a deterministic display spelling. Inferred replies are a leading-handle heuristic;
native reply and retweet metadata are unavailable.

Target 0 contains 19,370 lexically negative, 22,607 zero-polarity, and 1,023 positive tweets. Target
1 contains 843 negative, 2,692 zero-polarity, and 38,960 positive tweets. Figure 4 explicitly displays

associated with a positive-versus-nonpositive lexical split. It does not establish the label-generation
procedure, verified sentiment, harmfulness, or political homophily. English-oriented polarity can mis-

probabilitiesfrombothclassesbeforeadoption,potentiallyatdifferentsteps. Asuccessfulcompetition-
resolution event is narrower: adoption occurs at a step where both aggregate probabilities are simul-
taneously positive. These are model opportunities and resolutions, not observed Twitter contacts.

classes is 0.05896398% (95% CI [0.05819555,0.05973242]%), with denominator 17,598−50 = 17,548.
The mean actual resolution count is 0.0755 per run ([0.063503,0.087497]). Mean ever-both exposure

9.1 Prediction cohort and rolling-origin protocol
WithineachMonday-startUTCweek,Target-0andTarget-1tweetcountsdetermineauser’sdominant
operational label; ties are excluded. The saved label set contains 77,247 user-weeks, including 1,500
ties. Retaining non-tie labels with strictly prior graph-feature rows yields 16,292 prediction records;
59,455 non-tie label records lack required historical graph features. This eligibility excludes cold starts
and accounts not yet present in reconstructed interaction history.
Evaluation covers 12,126 eligible test user-weeks from 6,889 unique users over 15 test weeks, 19 De-
cember 2022–27 March 2023 (Table 2). At each origin, the immediately preceding eligible week is
used for validation and earlier eligible weeks for training, subject to an eight-week minimum training
history. Scaling and class weights use training data; selection and early stopping use validation data.
Target-week interactions and labels are excluded from predictive inputs. Repeated users across weeks
are permitted because the task predicts future behavior of observed users rather than performance on
unseen accounts.
Six structural variables are available: prior in/out degree, in/out event strength, weighted PageRank,
and reverse PageRank. History-aware models additionally use the latest prior label, its availability
indicator, historical class proportions, prior label count, and elapsed weeks since that label. All
are calculated strictly before the prediction week. Persistence predicts the last available non-tied
prior label, with training-prevalence fallback; Majority uses the training class prevalence. Neural
pseudorandom seeds are 42–51, distinct from graph seed nodes. Simple baselines use seed 42.
9.2 Node-only and graph architectures
Logistic Regression and the MLP use structural features without graph aggregation. The original
GRU uses a direct six-feature GRU on strictly earlier structural-feature sequences with a separate
six-candidate validation grid. The aligned GRU uses raw weekly structural-feature sequences through
Linear(6,h), ReLU, GRU(h,h), dropout, and a binary head. It retains the graph experiment’s projec-
tion, GRU head, historical cutoffs, scaling, validation grid, early stopping, and random-seed protocol,
replacing graph aggregation with raw input sequences. These GRUs are distinct architectures and
controls.
Static GCN, GraphSAGE, and GAT aggregate prior interactionneighborhoods. Forward and reversed
GAT use different message directions; incoming aggregation along recorded edges does not indepen-
dently verify information flow. Temporal GCN applies weekly graph convolution, projection, and
a GRU to node states with fixed convolutional parameters. It is not a parameter-evolving graph-
convolution architecture. Matched feature-only and rewired controls assess aggregation under a com-
mon information boundary, but sampling and tuning choices remain implementation-specific.

10 Discussion
10.1 Restricted directed recurrence despite a large WCC
The 17,598-user WCC appears large, yet its largest SCC contains only 17 users and most accounts
lie outside the core’s IN/OUT regions. WCC membership describes undirected connectedness, while
directed recurrence describes a much narrower property. This difference restricts structural opportuni-
tiesforsustainedcirculation. Itdoesnotprovethattopologyalonecausesorpreventsrealinformation
spread.
10.2 Chronology further restricts potential reach
Textreconstructionsupplies129,604timestampedeventswhosepairoverlapwiththesuppliednetwork
is high. Within this proxy, a static path can fail because interactions occur in an unusable order. The
high-out-strength seed means retain 74.87% of static reach in recorded direction and 52.38% in the
layer-aware sensitivity, with different results for other strata and undefined ratios for static sinks.
Chronology therefore matters for reconstructed opportunity, while uncertain direction and incomplete
visible references limit the interpretation as exposure.
10.3 Tiny secondary spread and rare direct competition
Under the balanced full parameterization, 50 initial seeds generate only 0.2695 secondary adoptions
on average. The mean ever-both non-seed fraction is about 0.05896%, and successful competition
resolutionsaverage0.0755perrun. Thesimulatedprocessesarenear-independentunderthesesettings,
rather than a widespread competition for adopting nodes. Conditional sensitivity to transmission or
seedimbalancedoesnotestablishreal-worldclassdominance. Seed-inclusivetotalsandundefinedzero-
secondary outcomes must remain visible to avoid mistaking initialization for amplification or parity
for equivalence.
10.4 Diﬀicult weekly prediction with modest history signal
Graph-only balanced accuracy remains near 0.50, whereas Persistence and history-based models pro-
vide modest signal. Recent operational orientation is the most useful available information among the
tested inputs. The history-plus-reversed-GAT model does not improve over its matched history-plus-
structural MLP. This finding concerns the tested implementation, cohort, and information boundary;
it is not a universal rejection of graph methods. The 15-week evaluation supports restrained model
selection and metric-specific inference rather than claims that temporal complexity improves forecast-
ing.
11 Limitations
1. Interaction direction is inferred from author/reference structure rather than verified exposure or
content transmission. Native reply/retweet metadata are unavailable, and leading-handle inferred
replies are not platform reply records.
2. Only 16 text-supported retweet events are recovered. Their sparse topology and nine orientation-
labelled pairs preclude broad retweet behavioral or mixing conclusions.
3. Target is an operational dataset label. TextBlob consistency is not multilingual validation; exter-
nal/ongoing human validation has no final result included here.
22
4. Time-respecting paths use reconstructed text-visible interactions. Missing handles, quotations,
deleted content, and invisible interactions limit completeness. High pair overlap does not validate
all recovered timestamps or causal paths.
5. The normalized 17,636-user WCC is a matching sensitivity. The static WCC, full temporal end-
point universe, and eligible user-week forecasting cohort have different denominators and selection
mechanisms.
6. Simulation is uncalibrated to observed adoption. The static full-period graph and November fea-
ture cutoff do not define a prospective exposure process; finite-horizon results depend on specified
probabilities and initial seed nodes.
7. Secondary spread is extremely small in the tested balanced full setting, with many undefined non-
seed outcomes. Rare direct competition limits substantive conclusions about competition, and no
equivalence margin is justified.
8. Spectral diagnostics are sensitive to self-loops and the tiny recurrent core. Process assumptions
needed for an established epidemic threshold are unvalidated.
9. Forecastingusesonly15testweeks,repeatedusers,andpotentiallydependentweeklyobservations.
Bootstrap and moving-block intervals are approximate with this short series; there is no unseen-
user or cross-network performance claim.
10. Eligibility excludes cold-start user-weeks and users lacking prior reconstructed graph features.
Results apply to the declared eligible cohort rather than all active users.
11. Neighborhood sampling, direction choices, tuning, and distinct GRU sequence constructions limit
architectural attribution. A fixed-weight GRU-over-GCN does not test parameter evolution.
12. One empirical dataset is studied, with undocumented source-card discrepancy and Weight seman-
tics. External validity and causal contagion are not established.
12 Ethics, Data Governance, and Dual Use
Political tweet text and account handles can be sensitive despite public availability. Results are
reported as aggregate structural and operational-label patterns without assigning private attributes,
ideology, credibility, or causal influence to named individuals. The submission does not redistribute
raw tweet text. Access to underlying account-derived metadata should follow applicable platform
terms and governance requirements; the data-source attribution is not a redistribution licence.
The uncalibrated simulation does not justify targeting users or suppressing content. Any future
intervention research would require verified exposure semantics, independent label validation, and as-
sessment of disparate effects across communities. Aggregate uncertainty and limited external validity
should be retained when interpreting these analyses.
13 Reproducibility
The project contains reproducibility_package/ with archived scripts, configuration, recorded
software versions and environment specification, fixed pseudorandom seeds, input/output SHA-256
records, and analysis assumptions. Its table and figure manifests link derived tables and figure source
data stored in results/tables/ and results/figures/reviewer_corrected/. These linked files
are part of the local evidence record; the manifests alone do not embed every result file. Known
missing package-version or acquisition details are documented rather than invented.
Literal/normalized matching rules, typed direction assumptions, temporal traversal definitions, sim-
ulator parameters, and forecasting information boundaries are recorded explicitly. The simulation
23
uses random base 42 and 2,000 paired runs per configuration/direction; temporal summaries use 2,000
seed resamples; neural forecasting uses random seeds 42–51. The saved forecasting table preserves
cohort-specific inference procedures rather than pooling summaries. Derived numerical tables and
figure source CSVs used in this manuscript are included as supporting evidence, with provenance and
a manuscript input/output manifest. No model retraining is needed to typeset these saved results.
The clean manuscript archive contains source, bibliography, referenced figures, and supporting de-
rived evidence. It is a typesetting package and does not by itself establish executable reproducibility.
Raw tweet text is excluded. No public repository, DOI for this project’s artifacts, or redistribution
licence is claimed. Authoritative input hashes appear in Table 1, and the local reproducibility package
documents the additional input requirements.
14 Conclusion
The directed Indian political Twitter network has a large weak component but a 17-user strongly con-
nected core. Reconstructed interaction chronology further restricts reach relative to static paths, with
seed-stratumanddirectionalsensitivity. Underthetestedbalancedfullsimulation,secondaryadoption
istinyandactualcompetitionisrare; initialseedcountsshouldnotbeinterpretedascascadeamplifica-
tion. Weekly graph-only forecasting is near chance, while Persistence and historical orientation supply
modest signal. Reversed graph attention does not improve over the matched history-plus-structural
MLP. These conditional findings support careful separation of topology, reconstructed opportunity,
simulated adoption, and prediction, without claiming causal contagion, class dominance, or broad
graph-model superiority.
