## What was changed in the polished source

The research findings, equations, numerical results, tables, figure references, and citations were preserved. The edit focuses on prose style:

- Replaced repetitive `This study / The evidence / The result` constructions with direct explanations.
- Varied sentence length and paragraph rhythm.
- Replaced generic academic transitions with concrete reasoning tied to the analysis.
- Used first-person singular selectively where the author made an explicit analysis choice (`I reconstructed`, `I use`, `I interpret`).
- Reduced repeated disclaimer templates while keeping the scientific limitations.
- Rewrote the abstract as a narrative of the three research questions instead of a compressed list of claims.
- Rewrote the introduction and discussion around the actual reasoning behind the project.
- Clarified matching, reconstructed-edge denominators, label validation, temporal reachability, simulation interpretation, and forecasting in ordinary academic prose.
- Removed the unsupported word `predeclared` from paired-comparison wording.
- Replaced `precision-style reconstructed-pair overlap` with the clearer `reconstructed-pair precision`.
- Removed the sentence-ending period attached directly to the second SHA-256 hash.

## Important before resubmission

1. Read every rewritten paragraph and change any sentence that does not sound like how you would explain the work.
2. Be prepared to explain every numerical result, method choice, and limitation in an evaluation or viva.
3. Compile the polished `.tex` inside the existing final manuscript folder so the current figures, tables, `evidence_numbers.tex`, and bibliography remain available.
4. Do not change equations, results, or citations merely to affect an AI detector score.
5. No one can guarantee an AI-detection score of 0%. Treat the score as a screening signal, not a factual measurement of authorship.
